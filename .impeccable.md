## Design Context

Source of truth: `brand-identity/brand-guidelines.md` (PharmIQ Brand Style Guide v2) and the tokens in
`frontend/src/theme/`. This section summarises them for design and audit skills.

### Users

- Australian community pharmacy staff: independent pharmacists, pharmacy owners, store managers,
  and assistants in multi-site networks.
- They use the app during a working shift and are often interrupted ("the distracted pharmacist").
  Core jobs: scan stock for expiry dates (camera or handheld PDT scanner) during store walks, review
  expiring and expired stock, write off expired items, claim supplier credits, run markdowns, import
  stock via CSV, and manage users, store areas and the subscription.
- Devices: desktop and tablet in the dispensary, phones and rugged handheld PDT devices on the shop
  floor. Handheld screens are small and used one-handed, sometimes with gloves.

### Brand Personality

- Expert infrastructure partner: calm, sophisticated, authoritative, reliable.
- "Clinical dignity": no playful, gamey or decorative UI. Clarity over noise.
- Voice: precise and clinical, short and direct. Use "pharmacy operations" and "your dispensary",
  not "healthcare journey".

### Aesthetic Direction

- Light mode is primary for the product (white `canvas`, navy `ink` `#0F172A`).
- Brand teal `#0F766E` is the primary action color. Amber `#D97706` means real urgency (expiry
  warnings) and is scarce. Both lose meaning when used as decoration.
- Typography: Outfit for UI headings, Inter for body and data (tabular numerals), JetBrains Mono
  for codes. Fraunces display serif is for marketing surfaces, not dense product screens.
- Depth from the surface ladder (`canvas` > `surface-1` > `surface-2`) and 1px hairline borders.
  Shadows are a last resort.
- Dashboard surfaces are information-dense (20-24px card padding). No hero metrics, gradient text,
  glassmorphism or other generic AI-dashboard patterns.

### Design Principles

1. Surface-led hierarchy, not decorative chrome.
2. Scarcity creates signal: teal and amber only where they carry meaning.
3. Data is the hero: tables, dates and counts must be easy to scan.
4. Speed for interrupted users: fewest steps, clear state, obvious next action.
5. Accessible by default: touch targets of 44px or more, body text of 16px or more, color is never
   the only signal, reduced motion is respected, WCAG AA contrast.
