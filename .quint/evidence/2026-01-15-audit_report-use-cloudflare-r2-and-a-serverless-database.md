Reliability is high (1.00) based on external validation of its components. The main advantage and risk are tied to its relative newness compared to AWS. While Cloudflare is a major player, R2 and the serverless database ecosystem are less mature than the AWS equivalents. This presents a risk of hitting edge cases or having a smaller community to rely on for support. The primary benefit is the significant cost reduction on egress fees. No bias detected.
